Help Articles

Browse useful information about your CRM.

To create a lead, open the Leads section and use the option for adding a new lead. Enter the customer's contact details, source, temperature, priority and assigned employee. Save the lead to add it to the sales pipeline.

Managers and administrators can assign leads to sales employees. The assigned employee becomes responsible for contacting the customer, recording activities and completing follow-ups.

A follow-up or task can contain an action, responsible employee, due date, priority and status. Pending tasks can become overdue when their due date passes. This helps the sales team track the next action for every customer.

Leads move through stages such as New, Contacted, Qualified, Proposal and Negotiation. The final outcomes are Won or Lost. Moving a lead through the pipeline helps the team understand its current sales position.

When a lead is successfully converted, the system can create a customer record and an associated deal. This allows the business to continue managing the customer and sales opportunity after the lead stage.

Reports provide visibility into pipeline stages, lead sources and sales employee activity. Managers can use this information to monitor the sales process and understand current workload and performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common CRM questions.

Lead temperature indicates the current level of sales interest. Hot leads have high priority, while Warm and Cold leads represent lower levels of immediate interest.

Leads can be assigned to sales employees. Managers and administrators can manage lead assignments according to their access permissions.

A successful lead can be converted into a customer and associated deal so the sales process can continue beyond the lead stage.

Tasks contain due dates and statuses. Pending tasks whose due date has passed can be identified as overdue and followed up by the responsible employee.

Company configuration such as company information, timezone and currency is managed through the Settings section according to user permissions.
